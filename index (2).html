Privacy Policy – AuraFly

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AuraFly (“we”, “our”, or “us”), a mobile game, handles information when you use the app.

1. No Account Required

AuraFly does not require you to create an account, register, or log in to play. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, or phone number to use the game.

2. Information and Advertising

We do not directly ask you to submit personal information. AuraFly uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • Device information and advertising identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate information derived from it;
  • Ad views, interactions, and advertising diagnostics; and
  • App performance or crash information, where supported by the services used.

The information collected and how it is used can depend on your device settings, the ad features enabled, and applicable consent requirements.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Game Progress and Local Storage

Game progress, coins, high scores, unlocked vehicles, and preferences may be stored locally on your device so they remain available when you close and reopen the app. Uninstalling AuraFly or clearing its app data may delete this locally stored information and reset your progress. Progress is not guaranteed to be recoverable.

4. Third-Party Services

AuraFly uses third-party services, including Google AdMob, to provide advertisements. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review the policies linked above.

5. Children’s Privacy

AuraFly does not ask users to provide personal information directly. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and consent settings will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements and the app’s selected audience settings.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app and its services. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
